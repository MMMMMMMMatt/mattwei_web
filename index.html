Matt Wei

M.S.E. Mechanical Engineering · Johns Hopkins University

I'm a robotics researcher and hardware engineer with a passion for bio-inspired systems, haptic interfaces, and clinically deployable medical robotics. My work spans snake locomotion analysis in the Terradynamics Lab, haptic device development in the Intuitive Computing Lab at JHU, and wearable health monitoring systems. I hold multiple utility patents and have published at IEEE Haptics Symposium 2024. Currently applying for PhD programs in robotics and mechanical intelligence.
